DOWNLOADED ON FIRST USE (NOT INCLUDED IN THIS INSTALLER)
--------------------------------------------------------
MeloTTS downloads its language checkpoints (myshell-ai/MeloTTS-*, MIT) and
BERT language models from Hugging Face the first time a language is used.
These are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AditsVoiceStudio\cache and are covered by their
own licenses, shown on each model's Hugging Face page.
